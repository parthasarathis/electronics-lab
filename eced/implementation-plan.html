How to use the course

Begin with the prerequisite concepts, predict a result, change a parameter in a live lesson, and follow its worked calculation. Use Lab Preparation to connect the concept with your assigned experiment.

Live lessons use ideal or explicitly simplified models. Compare assumptions with the component data, simulation model and instrument conditions used in the laboratory.
